fixes 

Face and eye detailer (you already have the tools). Impact Pack is installed. Add a FaceDetailer node with a face_yolov8m bbox detector, which comes with Impact Subpack. It crops the face, redraws it at full resolution, and pastes it back. Use denoise 0.35-0.5. Since your model chain uses the DMD2 LoRA, keep 8 steps and CFG 1 in the detailer too.
Hands. Same idea with a hand detector (hand_yolov8s) in a second Detailer. Use a slightly lower denoise (0.3-0.4) so it fixes the fingers without inventing a new hand.
Feet. There’s no standard feet detector in Impact Subpack. Two options: mask the feet by hand and inpaint them with your ComfyUI-Inpaint-CropAndStitch node, or look for a feet/foot YOLO detector on CivitAI. I haven’t verified that a good one exists. For inpainting, prompt only for the feet, for example high heels, ankle strap, toes, foot, detailed.
Hires pass. After the first generation, upscale 1.5x and run a second sampler at denoise 0.35-0.45. This improves everything at once.
Drop the DMD2 LoRA for the final pass. Speed LoRAs lose some fine detail. For finished images, try 28 steps, CFG 5.5, no DMD2 on the detailer passes.
Try several seeds. Hands and feet are the hardest parts, and you’ll often need 4-8 attempts to get a good base.
